Issue: **[#759](https://github.com/DotNaos/project-space/issues/759)**
Requirements: **Not applicable**

## Summary

Make retained archive age evidence deterministic in retention collector tests while
leaving production retention behavior unchanged.

## Problem

The fixtures used a fixed collector clock but retained archive directories kept their
real filesystem modification times. As the wall clock moved past the fixture clock, the
collector deferred otherwise eligible archives and made replacement and recovery cases
fail for reasons unrelated to the behavior under test.

## Scope

- Give retained archive fixtures a fixed modification time before collector inspection.
- Restore a fixed collector clock that is later than the archive evidence.
- Re-run the race-enabled collector cases and repository gates.

## Out of scope

- Changing production minimum-age policy or retention defaults.
- Changing inode, replacement, hardlink, crash-recovery, or fail-closed checks.
- Changing PR #755 or issue #754.

## Delivery

This test-only correction ships through the normal pull request and patch-release flow.
It needs no migration, Preview surface, or Production deployment.

## Requirements rationale

Requirements are not applicable because the change only makes test fixture timestamps
deterministic. It does not add, remove, or change supported product or operational
behavior.
