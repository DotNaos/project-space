# Persist task handoffs and execution identity

Project Space now has a durable, provider-neutral foundation for handing a Task
to an implementation agent. Structured Handoff revisions remain immutable, and
each Task Execution records its exact Task, Environment, connector generation,
workspace, repository, branch, commit, and executor binding.

Retries are protected by a durable operation ledger, shared Environment capacity
is leased safely, and uncertain work remains fenced until it is reconciled.
Archive and retention rules preserve audit history without storing credentials,
device codes, transcripts, raw workspace paths, or unrestricted URLs.
